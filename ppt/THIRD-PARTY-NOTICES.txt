The gallery adapts the camera/depth interaction, effect01's six-frame clipping
sequence, and cylindrical carousel geometry in native JavaScript/CSS3D. It does
not bundle the references' artworks or third-party runtime libraries. Actual PPT
images are the site's supplied materials. A repeated transition frame is not an
additional slide or work. The overview now uses seeded recyclable spatial
regions; repeated appearances map to the same six real decks and 23 real pages.
